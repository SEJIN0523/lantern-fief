This task makes ALL the art for a fantasy village-building + auto-battle dungeon mobile game. Story: Sejin, a tired Korean office worker in his 30s, is hit by a car and wakes in a fantasy forest, saves a village child from goblins, and ends up developing a poor mountain village of a kingdom while exploring dungeons to find his way home.

Use the attached image as the strict style reference for EVERY image in this task: chunky pixel art, dark 1px outline, flat shading with 2-3 tones per color, warm earthy palette. Characters: chibi-ish proportions (about 1:3.5 head to body), full body, 3/4 view, standing combat-ready pose, same scale and framing as the reference. Scenes and backgrounds use the same pixel density, outline and palette so characters fit on them.

Generate ALL images listed below in one run, one file per item, at the stated path. Work through the whole list without stopping.

==================================================
PART 1 — PLAYABLE CHARACTERS (51 sheets, saved as output/jobs/<file>.png)

SHEET FORMAT (all character, NPC and monster sheets in this task):
- Canvas exactly 1536x1024, 3 columns x 2 rows = 6 cells of 512x512. Cell order: top-left = slot 1, top-middle = slot 2, top-right = slot 3, bottom-left = slot 4, bottom-middle = slot 5, bottom-right = slot 6.
- One figure per cell, centered, all figures the same height and scale, feet on the same baseline in each row, nothing crossing cell borders.
- Background: solid flat #FF00FF magenta everywhere, no gradient, no texture, no floor, no ground shadow. Never use magenta or hot pink inside the figures.
- No text, no labels, no numbers, no UI, no frames.
- Player characters and NPCs face RIGHT. Monsters face LEFT.
- All playable characters are adults.

IDENTITY RULE (very important): each job line has 6 fixed people (identities). The person in slot N must be the SAME person (same face, race, gender, skin tone, hair color and hairstyle, body type) on every sheet of that line; only the outfit, weapon and effects change with the job. Promoting a character must look like the same person in new gear.

TIER RULE:
- Tier 1 = cheap worn leather/cloth, plain weapon.
- Tier 2 = better fitted gear, some metal, one accent color.
- Tier 3 = elite ornate gear with gold or silver trim and glowing or enchanted weapon accents.
- Hidden = tier 3 quality with a unique silhouette and one distinctive magical motif.
